Assumptions and sources
  • Real, from NASA or Launch Library 2: instrument masses and powers as quoted, planetary constants, launch vehicle data. Launch Library 2 is not a NASA source.
  • Game values: prices other than launch, array and battery masses, reliability model, science points and the survey goals. Where a source gives no power, a default is used (marked GAME).
  • Simplifications: coverage is a scalar progress counter, not a mapped survey; the survey does not use real NASA measurements yet.
  • Contract needs: magnetic vector + electron spectrum.
