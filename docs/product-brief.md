# Space Mission Design Game: product brief and 30-second script

**Live:** https://kaungkhantko26.github.io/spacemissiondesigngame/  ·  **Built for:** NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

## One line
Design a spacecraft, launch it from a real spaceport, fly it to the Moon, then run its science and see what your design choices did, using real NASA data.

## What you do (one mission, four steps)
1. **Design** (Mission Architect): pick the Moon, a real launch vehicle, propulsion, power (solar and RTG), an antenna, and real NASA instruments. The review panel checks mass, delta-v, power, data link and budget live. Instruments are matched by what they measure (a magnetometer and an electron reflectometer are needed for the lunar survey). Then choose up to three regions to survey on a map of real Lunar Prospector magnetic-field data.
2. **Launch** (Launch Control): fly the ascent from a real spaceport (Kennedy, Vandenberg, Kourou, Sriharikota, Baikonur). Three difficulties: Cadet (autopilot), Pilot (you steer, guided cue), Commander (everything manual). Saturn V flies the full Apollo-style profile: parking orbit, translunar injection in the window, coast, lunar-orbit insertion.
3. **Operate** (Mission Control): from lunar orbit, manage power through eclipse, point the spacecraft, observe, downlink, and decide anomalies (solar storm, navigation error and more). Time is saved; refresh and resume.
4. **Learn** (report): your survey drawn on the real map, a graded scorecard with cause and effect, a "What we can learn from this" section, and a failure coach that explains what went wrong and what to do next time.

## What is real, and what is simplified
- **Real NASA / public data:** Lunar Prospector magnetometer Level 4 field map at 37 km (NASA Planetary Data System, Planetary Plasma Interactions node); NASA NSSDCA instrument masses and powers; planetary constants; Launch Library 2 launch-vehicle data (not NASA); Saturn V stage data.
- **Game rules (labelled in the UI):** prices besides launch, array and battery masses, reliability model, science points, the coverage model and the operations balance.
- Shared instrument packages are counted once; every instrument card states what its mass figure covers and links to its NASA catalog entry.
- Players practise a historical survey; they do not collect new NASA observations.

## Features worth showing
- Real 3D astronaut (customisable: suits, patterns, helmets, flag incl. Myanmar and NASA marks, patches, backpacks), shareable crew ID card (save as image or link).
- Textured 3D Earth, Moon, Mars and Sun; Apollo-style spacecraft model; cinematic Earth-to-Moon coast and lunar capture.
- Real recorded voice (ElevenLabs) for the countdown, flight callouts and Moon coast; synthesized engine and effects; mute switch.
- Guided spotlight tutorials (menu, Launch Control, manual flight school), keyboard shortcuts for every control, failure coaching.
- Team Up: crew roles and characters, then a shared flight.
- Works on phone, tablet and desktop; accessibility settings (reduce motion, high contrast, 3D quality).

## Honest limits (do not overclaim)
- No learner study has been run yet; no learning-gain numbers exist.
- Mars, telescope and landing missions are planned, not playable. Team Up results and some profile/progression data are still local or mock-backed.
- Physics and operations are simplified for teaching; this is not flight software.
