Launch Control

Roll out from a real spaceport and fly the ascent from the launch pad into orbit.

01Mission

Fly to orbit, or the full Apollo-style trip: parking orbit → wait for the TLI window → re-light the upper stage → coast to the Moon → lunar orbit.

02Difficulty

Each level changes who flies, how heavy the payload is, the weather, failures and how strict the limits are.

03Spaceport

Real launch pads from the dataset. The closer to the equator, the more free eastward speed you get from Earth's spin.

04Launch vehicle

Liftoff mass, thrust, size and capacity are Launch Library 2 data. Stages, Isp and structure are modelled (game values).

05Configure spacecraft

Tune the stack before rollout. Less payload or fuel makes the rocket lighter; a throttle limit makes the engines gentler. Too little fuel cannot reach orbit.
