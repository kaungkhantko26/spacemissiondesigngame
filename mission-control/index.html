Lunar Magnetic Survey

Map the Moon's magnetic anomalies from a polar orbit. You fly the mission yourself: manage power, data, heat and fuel for 30 days of science, and make the calls when things go wrong.

Mandatory

  • ▸ Reach lunar orbit
  • ▸ Carry a magnetometer (a “fields” instrument)
  • ▸ Survey ≥ 80 % of the surface in 30 days
  • ▸ Return ≥ 60 GB of useful data

Bonus

  • ▸ Return ≥ 150 GB
  • ▸ Finish with ≥ 60 % spacecraft health
  • ▸ Observe the unexpected anomaly

Budget $250M · Simplified, game-balanced operations model (not NASA-certified). Launch price, Δv and instruments come from the dataset.
